### Figure 1 Tree Plot of subspecies present in East Africa.
Tree plot considering subspecies present in East Africa, the tree was made by clustering principal component analysis results, which was performed using ~6,000,000 WGS SNPs. Labels are coloured according to the subspecies. Newly sequenced Rwenzori mountains samples are represented with roman number labels I – VII and RW.

### Figure 2
Pairwise FST comparison of East African monticola and scutellata populations. Blue and green colour codes were used to represent regions of increased genetic differentiation on chromosome 7 and chromosome 9 respectively. a) Genome-Wide FST comparison of highland (n = 38) and lowland (n = 25) honey bees the black line is an average of sliding windows of 1000 non overlapping SNPs. b) and c) Zoom-in of chromosome 7 and chromosome 9 with the relative linkage disequilibrium heatmaps. Heatmaps were generated by calculating one r^2 value every 50 SNPs using the LDHeatmap R package (Shin, et al., 2006).
